EMPLOYMENT AGREEMENT

This Employment Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the “Agreement”)
is entered into on this 15th day of July, 2026.

BY AND BETWEEN

TechNova Solutions Private Limited, a company incorporated under the
Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 5th Floor, Orion
Tech Park, Whitefield Main Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka – 560066,
hereinafter referred to as the “Company” or “Employer”, which expression
shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include its
successors, administrators, and permitted assigns;

AND

Ms. Priya Nair, daughter of Mr. Ramesh Nair, aged 27 years, residing at
Flat No. 405, Silver Heights Apartments, HSR Layout, Bengaluru,
Karnataka – 560102, hereinafter referred to as the “Employee”, which
expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof,
include her legal heirs and permitted assigns.

WHEREAS

• The Employer is engaged in the business of software development, cloud
computing services, and technology consulting. • The Employer has agreed
to employ the Employee, and the Employee has agreed to accept such
employment upon the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements
contained herein, the parties agree as follows:

1.  Interpretation

For the purposes of this Agreement, the following expressions shall have
the meanings assigned to them:

a.  “Company”

Means TechNova Solutions Private Limited and includes its successors and
permitted assigns.

b.  “Confidential Information”

Means all proprietary, technical, financial, commercial, customer,
software, product, and business information belonging to the Company
that is not publicly available.

2.  Position

a.  The Employee is appointed as Software Engineer in the Technology
    Division of the Company.

b.  The Employee shall report directly to the Engineering Manager or any
    other person designated by the Company.

c.  The Company may reasonably modify the Employee’s duties, reporting
    structure, or work location based on business requirements.

3.  Term and Probation

a.  Employment shall commence on 15th July, 2026.

b.  The first six (6) months shall constitute the probation period.

c.  During probation, either party may terminate employment by giving
    seven (7) days’ written notice.

d.  Upon successful completion of probation, the Employee shall become a
    confirmed employee subject to the Company’s policies.

4.  Duties and Responsibilities

a.  The Employee shall devote her full working time and attention to the
    business of the Company.

b.  The Employee shall perform assigned duties diligently, honestly,
    maintain professional conduct, and comply with all lawful
    instructions issued by the Employer.

5.  Working Hours and Place of Work

a.  The Employee shall ordinarily work from Monday to Friday between
    9:30 A.M. and 6:30 P.M., subject to reasonable changes as determined
    by the Company.

b.  The Employee’s primary place of work shall be the Company’s
    Bengaluru office unless otherwise communicated by the Employer.

c.  The Company may permit remote or hybrid working arrangements based
    on business requirements and applicable Company policies. Such
    arrangements may be modified or withdrawn at the Company’s
    discretion.

6.  Compensation

a.  The Employee shall receive an annual Cost to Company (CTC) of INR
    8,40,000 (Rupees Eight Lakh Forty Thousand Only).

b.  Salary shall be paid monthly through electronic bank transfer after
    statutory deductions.

c.  The Employee shall be eligible for annual performance reviews and
    salary revisions at the discretion of the Company.

7.  Leave and Benefits

a.  The Employee shall be entitled to 18 days of paid annual leave and
    12 days of sick leave per calendar year.

b.  The Employee shall also be entitled to public holidays declared by
    the Company.

8.  Confidentiality

a.  During the course of employment, the Employee may have access to
    Confidential Information belonging to the Company.

b.  The Employee agrees not to disclose, reproduce, copy, or use such
    Confidential Information except for the proper performance of
    official duties.

c.  This obligation shall continue even after termination of employment.

9.  Data Protection and Information Security

a.  The Employee shall comply with all Company policies relating to
    information security, data protection, and acceptable use of
    information technology resources.

b.  The Employee shall take reasonable precautions to prevent
    unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or loss of Company
    data.

c.  Any actual or suspected data breach or security incident shall be
    reported to the Company immediately upon becoming aware of it.

10. Intellectual Property

a.  All software, documentation, inventions, designs, source code,
    reports, and work products created by the Employee during the course
    of employment shall remain the exclusive property of the Company.

b.  The Employee agrees to execute all documents necessary to transfer
    intellectual property rights to the Company.

11. Non-Solicitation

a.  During the Employee’s employment and for a period of six (6) months
    following the termination of employment, the Employee shall not,
    directly or indirectly, solicit or induce any employee of the
    Company to leave the Company’s employment.

b.  During the same period, the Employee shall not solicit or attempt to
    solicit any client or customer of the Company with whom the Employee
    had material business dealings during the course of employment for
    the purpose of providing competing services.

c.  Nothing in this clause shall prevent the Employee from accepting
    employment with another organization, provided that the Employee
    complies with the confidentiality and intellectual property
    obligations contained in this Agreement.

12. Termination

a.  Either party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30)
    days’ written notice after confirmation of employment.

b.  The Company may terminate employment immediately in cases involving
    fraud, misconduct, theft, breach of confidentiality, or other
    serious violations of Company policy.

c.  Upon termination, the Employee shall immediately return all Company
    property, identification cards, laptops, documents, and confidential
    materials.

13. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

a.  This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.

b.  Any dispute arising from this Agreement shall first be resolved
    through mutual discussion.

c.  If the dispute remains unresolved, it shall be referred to
    arbitration under the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.

d.  The seat of arbitration shall be Bengaluru, Karnataka.

14. Entire Agreement

This Agreement constitutes the complete understanding between the
Employer and the Employee concerning employment and supersedes all prior
communications, negotiations, and understandings.

Any amendment shall be valid only if made in writing and signed by both
parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF

The parties have executed this Agreement on the day and year first above
written.
