SERVICE AGREEMENT
This Service Agreement (hereinafter referred to as the "Agreement") is entered into on this 10th day of September, 2026.
BY AND BETWEEN
TechNova Solutions Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 5th Floor, Orion Tech Park, Whitefield Main Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka – 560066, hereinafter referred to as the "Service Provider", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include its successors and permitted assigns;
AND
GreenLeaf Retail Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at 12, Business Plaza, MG Road, Bengaluru, Karnataka – 560001, hereinafter referred to as the "Client", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context or meaning thereof, include its successors and permitted assigns.
The Service Provider and the Client shall hereinafter collectively be referred to as the "Parties" and individually as a "Party."

WHEREAS
The Client desires to engage the Service Provider for the design, development, implementation, and maintenance of a custom web-based inventory management system.
The Service Provider represents that it possesses the necessary expertise, personnel, and technical capabilities required to perform the Services.
NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, the Parties hereby agree as follows:

1. Interpretation
For the purposes of this Agreement, the following terms shall have the meanings assigned to them:
a) "Services"
Means the software development, implementation, testing, deployment, maintenance, and related technical services to be provided by the Service Provider under this Agreement.
b) "Deliverables"
Means all reports, documentation, software modules, source code, user manuals, and other work products delivered to the Client.
c) "Project"
Means the development of a web-based inventory management system for the Client.

2. Scope of Services
a. The Service Provider shall design, develop, test, deploy, and maintain the Project in accordance with the specifications mutually agreed upon by the Parties.
b. The Services shall include requirement analysis, software development, quality assurance testing, deployment assistance, user training, and post-deployment technical support.
c. Any additional services requested by the Client outside the agreed scope shall require prior written approval and may result in additional charges.

3. Responsibilities of the Service Provider
a. The Service Provider shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care, and professional competence.
b. The Service Provider shall assign qualified personnel for execution of the Project.
c. The Service Provider shall provide periodic progress reports to the Client.
d. The Service Provider shall make reasonable efforts to complete the Project within the agreed timeline.

4. Responsibilities of the Client
a. The Client shall provide all information, documentation, approvals, and access reasonably required for the successful completion of the Project.
b. The Client shall review Deliverables and provide feedback within seven (7) business days of submission.
c. The Client shall ensure timely payment of all invoices issued under this Agreement.

5. Fees and Payment
a. The Client agrees to pay the Service Provider a total contract value of INR 12,50,000 (Rupees Twelve Lakh Fifty Thousand Only).
b. Payments shall be made according to the following schedule:
30% upon execution of this Agreement. 
40% upon successful completion of software development. 
30% upon final delivery and acceptance of the Project. 
c. Any payment delayed beyond 15 days from the invoice due date shall attract interest at 12% per annum until payment is received.

6. Confidentiality
a. During the course of this Agreement, either Party may disclose confidential business, technical, or commercial information.
b. Both Parties agree to maintain the confidentiality of such information and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent.
c. The confidentiality obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of five (5) years.

7. Intellectual Property
a. Upon receipt of full payment, all intellectual property rights relating to the final Deliverables specifically developed for the Client shall vest in the Client.
b. The Service Provider shall retain ownership of its pre-existing software libraries, development frameworks, methodologies, tools, and proprietary technologies used during the Project.
c. The Client shall not reproduce, modify, or distribute proprietary software belonging to the Service Provider without prior written permission.

8. Acceptance of Deliverables
a. The Client shall review and conduct acceptance testing of the Deliverables within a reasonable review period after delivery.
b. If the Deliverables do not materially conform to the agreed specifications, the Client shall notify the Service Provider in writing, stating the reasons for rejection and any requested revisions.
c. The Service Provider shall use reasonable efforts to address valid revision requests and resubmit the Deliverables for further review.
d. If the Client does not reject the Deliverables with written reasons within the applicable review period, the Deliverables shall be deemed accepted.

9. Term and Termination
a. This Agreement shall remain in force until completion of the Services unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Agreement.
b. Either Party may terminate this Agreement by providing thirty (30) days' written notice.
c. Either Party may terminate this Agreement immediately in the event of a material breach that remains unremedied for fifteen (15) days after written notice.
d. Upon termination, the Client shall pay for all Services satisfactorily performed up to the date of termination.

10. Limitation of Liability
a. Neither Party shall be liable for any indirect, incidental, consequential, or special damages arising out of this Agreement.
b. The aggregate liability of the Service Provider shall not exceed the total amount paid under this Agreement.

11. Warranty and Support
a. The Service Provider shall provide a reasonable post-delivery warranty period for the Deliverables.
b. During the warranty period, the Service Provider shall correct defects attributable to its work at no additional charge.
c. The Service Provider shall provide basic technical support during the warranty period in accordance with the terms of this Agreement.

12. Force Majeure
Neither Party shall be liable for any delay or failure in performing its obligations due to events beyond its reasonable control, including natural disasters, war, pandemics, government restrictions, cyber-attacks, or power failures.
The affected Party shall promptly notify the other Party and resume performance as soon as reasonably practicable.

13. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution
a. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of India.
b. The Parties shall first attempt to resolve disputes through mutual discussion and negotiation.
c. If the dispute cannot be resolved amicably within thirty (30) days, it shall be referred to arbitration in accordance with the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996.
d. The seat and venue of arbitration shall be Bengaluru, Karnataka, and the proceedings shall be conducted in the English language.

14. Entire Agreement
This Agreement constitutes the complete understanding between the Parties regarding the Services and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements.
No amendment or modification shall be valid unless made in writing and signed by authorized representatives of both Parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF
The Parties have executed this Agreement on the day and year first above written.
Date: 10th September, 2026
